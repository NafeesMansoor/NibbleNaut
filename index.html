    How to play

    • Eat smaller fish and food to grow. You can eat fish that are clearly smaller than you.
    • Avoid bigger fish. Dangerous fish have a red outline; hunters chasing you show a ! and edge arrows.
    • Dash to escape or snatch prey. It recharges in a couple of seconds.
    • Your score becomes points. Spend them in Upgrade fish on advanced fish that earn more points per bite.
    • Score well to earn a next-game reward: a bigger start, a speed boost, or the legendary Ocean of Plenty.
    Desktop WASD / arrow keys to swim (or hold the mouse) · Space to dash · Esc to pause
    Touch drag on the left to swim · tap DASH on the right
